    Where downloads come from

    A web page cannot reach a video host directly — browsers block it, and the stream URLs are signed. Something has to run yt-dlp for you. Pick what.

    Running yt-dlp on the machine you are sitting at is the shortest path: the video goes from the site straight to your disk, and nothing but this page comes from the internet. Browsers permit it because localhost counts as secure. On a phone this will not work — nothing is running on the phone. Use a deployed server there, or open your computer's own address in the phone's browser.
